Hoshinoma Support

For sign-in, subscriptions, account deletion, privacy, safety, or technical support, email xuzhikeji@gmail.com.

Please include your app version, iOS version, language, and a short description. Do not email identity tokens, session tokens, passwords, or sensitive conversation text.

Account deletion

Open Me → Account & sync → Delete account and cloud data. If the in-app flow is unavailable, contact support from the email address above.

Safety

To report unsafe or inappropriate AI-generated content, open that conversation's settings and choose Report a reply. Your email app will open with a reference to the fictional character. Hoshinoma does not attach conversation text automatically; include only the minimum excerpt needed to investigate. Safety reports are reviewed through the support inbox.

Hoshinoma is fictional entertainment and is not an emergency or medical service. If you or someone else may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services and a trusted person nearby.

Subscriptions

Purchases and renewals are handled by Apple. Use Restore Purchases inside Hoshinoma if an active entitlement is not shown, and manage or cancel the subscription from your Apple ID subscription settings.

删除账号

打开我的 → 账号与同步 → 删除账号和云端数据。如果 App 内流程不可用，请通过上述邮箱联系支持。

安全举报

如需举报不安全或不当的 AI 生成内容，请打开对应聊天的设置并选择举报回复。App 不会自动附带聊天正文；请只提供调查所需的最少片段。举报将通过支持邮箱处理。

订阅

购买与续订由 Apple 处理。如果有效权益没有显示，请在 Hoshinoma 内使用恢复购买；订阅的管理或取消请前往 Apple ID 订阅设置。
