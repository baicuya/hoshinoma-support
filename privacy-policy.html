Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Hoshinoma is an AI-assisted fictional campus companion app. This policy explains what information is processed, why it is needed, and the choices available to you. Hoshinoma does not sell personal data, use it for cross-app advertising, or track you across apps or websites for advertising.

Information we process

  • Account: an identifier from the sign-in option you choose, name and email address when provided by that option, and the session information needed to keep you signed in.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose Google sign-in, its SDK may collect your name, email address, phone number, approximate location (which may be inferred from your IP address), user ID, device ID, usage data, and other data. The SDK declares name, email, phone number, and approximate location for app functionality; user ID and other data for app functionality and analytics; and device ID and usage data for analytics. It declares all these data linked to you and not used for tracking.
  • Your content: messages, an optional display name and photo, character choices, conversation memories, mailbox state, relationship progress, and content you submit to support.
  • Voice: recordings you deliberately send for transcription. Raw recordings are handled temporarily and are not intentionally retained after processing.
  • Purchases: subscription product, entitlement, transaction status, and expiration information supplied by Apple. We do not receive your full payment-card details.
  • Technical information: app version, language, request status, and limited security or diagnostic data needed to operate and protect the service.

How we use information

We use this information to create and protect your account, generate and deliver conversations and voice features, sync your progress, provide subscriptions, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, and improve reliability. AI-generated content can be inaccurate; do not send information you do not want processed for these features.

Service providers and sharing

Information may be processed by trusted providers that support authentication and sign-in analytics, cloud hosting and security, AI and voice processing, App Store payments, and website delivery. These providers may process data in countries other than your own under their applicable safeguards. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect users and the service.

Storage and retention

Account-linked conversations and progress are kept until you delete the relevant content or your account. Raw voice recordings are temporary. Limited operational records are retained only as long as reasonably needed for security, reliability, legal, and fraud-prevention purposes. Sign-in providers may retain their own records under their policies; deleting your Hoshinoma account does not delete those provider records.

Your choices

You can use text without sending voice, clear conversations and memories in the app, manage your subscription in Apple ID settings, and delete your account and associated cloud data from Me → Account & sync. You can also contact support for access, correction, or deletion help.

Children and safety

Hoshinoma is not directed to children under 13. All fictional student characters are adults. Hoshinoma is entertainment and is not an emergency, medical, or mental-health service.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encrypted network connections and account-scoped access. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the product changes. Material updates will be reflected by a new effective date and, when appropriate, an in-app notice.
