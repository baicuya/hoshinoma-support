Hoshinoma Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026 · Operator: Xiaodong Jiang, independent developer

Hoshinoma is an AI-assisted fictional campus companion app. This policy explains what data the app processes, why it is needed, which service providers receive it, and how you can delete it. Hoshinoma is not directed to children under 13, and every fictional student character is an adult university student.

1. Account data

You must choose Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In before using the app. Hoshinoma receives a stable provider account identifier and exchanges the provider credential for an opaque Hoshinoma session. Hoshinoma does not intentionally store your Apple or Google password. Google profile names and email addresses are not stored in Hoshinoma's account database.

2. Content you provide

  • Chat messages, mailbox state, relationship progress, and generated chat memories.
  • An optional teacher display name and optional profile photo.
  • Voice recordings only when you hold the voice button and choose to send them.
  • Language, fictional student selection, local time, and a pseudonymous request or session identifier needed to deliver the feature.

3. AI processing and explicit consent

After you review the in-app disclosure and consent, Hoshinoma sends the minimum content needed for the feature to its Cloudflare-hosted service. Chat text, the selected fictional character, your optional display name, and relevant chat memories may then be processed by Alibaba Cloud DashScope to generate a reply. Voice recordings may be processed for transcription, and reply text may be processed for speech synthesis.

Hoshinoma does not use this data for advertising, data brokerage, or cross-app tracking. AI output may be inaccurate. Do not submit information that you do not want processed by these services.

4. Service providers

  • Apple — optional identity provider through Sign in with Apple.
  • Google — optional identity provider through Google Sign-In.
  • Cloudflare — application API, security controls, rate limiting, and database hosting.
  • Alibaba Cloud DashScope — text generation, speech recognition, and speech synthesis.

Provider processing is also governed by the applicable Cloudflare Privacy Policy, Alibaba Cloud Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy, and Apple Privacy Policy. Data may be processed in countries outside your place of residence, including the United States, Singapore, and China, subject to the providers' contractual and legal safeguards.

5. Subscriptions and purchases

If you purchase a subscription, Apple processes the payment and provides Hoshinoma with a verified product entitlement, transaction status, and expiration information. Hoshinoma does not receive your full payment-card details. Purchases are also governed by Apple's terms and privacy practices. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID subscription settings.

6. Storage and retention

  • Account-linked conversations, profile data, mailbox state, and relationship progress remain in Hoshinoma's database until you delete the account or delete the relevant content.
  • Raw voice recordings are read from a temporary file, sent for transcription, and deleted from the device immediately after reading. Hoshinoma does not intentionally persist raw voice recordings in its database or application logs.
  • Operational logs contain limited metadata such as result status, model, language, fictional student identifier, duration, and rate-limit outcome. They are configured not to contain chat text, identity tokens, or Hoshinoma session tokens and are retained only for the infrastructure provider's configured operational retention period.
  • On-device speech audio generated from a fictional student's reply may be cached and can be removed by deleting the app.

7. Your choices and rights

You can avoid voice processing by using text chat. You can clear individual conversations and memories inside the app. In Me → Account & sync, you can delete your account and associated cloud data directly. Account deletion removes Hoshinoma account records, active sessions, synced content, and the account-scoped copy on the current device, except data that must be kept to comply with law.

You may withdraw consent by deleting your account and discontinuing the online features. To request access, correction, or assistance with deletion, contact us through the support page below.

8. Support, TestFlight, and this website

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include and use it to respond, investigate safety reports, and troubleshoot. Please avoid sending sensitive conversation text unless it is necessary. If you use a TestFlight build, Apple may make beta diagnostics or feedback available under Apple's terms.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and contains no developer-added analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. GitHub may process network information for security and delivery as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. Security

Hoshinoma uses TLS in transit, Keychain storage for the opaque app session, hashed session tokens on the server, account-scoped database access, input limits, and rate limiting. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes

Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date and, when appropriate, a new in-app consent request.

11. Contact

Email: xuzhikeji@gmail.com · Hoshinoma Support

Hoshinoma 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 18 日 · 运营者：姜晓东（独立开发者）

Hoshinoma 是一款使用 AI 的虚构校园陪伴应用。本政策说明 App 处理哪些数据、处理目的、接收数据的服务提供商以及删除方式。App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，所有虚构学生角色均为成年大学生。

1. 账号数据

使用 App 前必须选择 Apple 登录或 Google 登录。Hoshinoma 接收稳定的身份提供商账号标识，并将身份凭据交换为不透明的 Hoshinoma 会话。Hoshinoma 不保存 Apple 或 Google 密码，也不会在账号数据库中主动保存 Google 个人资料姓名或邮箱。

2. 你提供的内容

  • 聊天消息、信箱状态、关系进度与自动生成的聊天回忆。
  • 可选的老师昵称与头像。
  • 仅在按住语音按钮并选择发送时产生的语音录音。
  • 为提供功能所需的语言、虚构学生标识、本地时间以及化名化的请求或会话标识。

3. AI 处理与明确同意

你在 App 内阅读说明并同意后，Hoshinoma 会把实现功能所需的最少内容发送到托管于 Cloudflare 的服务。聊天文本、所选虚构角色、可选昵称与相关聊天回忆可能会由阿里云百炼 DashScope 处理以生成回复；语音录音可能用于转写，回复文本可能用于语音合成。

Hoshinoma 不会将这些数据用于广告、数据交易或跨 App 跟踪。AI 输出可能不准确，请不要提交不希望这些服务处理的信息。

4. 服务提供商与跨境处理

服务提供商包括 Apple、Google、Cloudflare 与阿里云百炼 DashScope。数据可能在你居住地以外的国家或地区处理，包括美国、新加坡和中国，并受相应提供商的合同与法律保护措施约束。上方英文版本提供了各服务商隐私政策链接。

5. 订阅与购买

如果你购买订阅，付款由 Apple 处理，Hoshinoma 会读取经验证的商品权益、交易状态与到期信息，但不会收到完整的银行卡资料。你可以在 Apple ID 的订阅设置中管理或取消订阅。

6. 保存期限

  • 账号关联的对话、个人资料、信箱状态与关系进度会保存至你删除账号或删除相应内容。
  • 原始录音从临时文件读取并发送转写后，会立即从设备删除；Hoshinoma 不会主动将原始录音写入数据库或应用日志。
  • 运行日志仅包含结果状态、模型、语言、虚构学生标识、耗时及限流结果等必要元数据，不包含聊天正文、身份令牌或 Hoshinoma 会话令牌，仅按基础设施服务商配置的运行保留期保存。

7. 你的控制与权利

你可以只使用文字聊天，也可以在 App 内清除单段对话与聊天回忆。在我的 → 账号与同步中，可以直接删除账号与相关云端数据。除法律要求保留的数据外，删除会移除账号记录、会话、同步内容与当前设备上的账号范围数据。

8. 支持、TestFlight 与本网站

当你发送支持邮件时，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和主动提供的内容，用于回复、处理安全举报和排查问题。请仅提供必要的聊天片段。TestFlight 可能依据 Apple 的条款提供测试诊断或反馈。

本网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，没有加入开发者侧分析、广告或追踪 Cookie。GitHub 可能为安全与内容分发处理网络信息。
